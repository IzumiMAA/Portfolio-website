About me

Hi! Welcome to my creative workspace — part code lab, part design studio, always evolving. I build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript Python, C++, Java, and C#, shaping ideas into functional websites and applications. On the design side, my toolkit includes Figma for sleek prototypes, Adobe Photoshop for photo magic, and Adobe Illustrator for crisp vector art. Together, they help me craft interfaces that feel modern, intuitive, and user‑friendly. This portfolio is my ongoing project — a mix of polished pieces and experiments, where development meets design. Expect clean layouts, thoughtful UI/UX touches, and a few playful detours along the way. Everything here is a work in progress, but that’s the fun of it.
